# Who pays for midnight.vote

- Status: working hypothesis, 29 September 2026. No customer has paid yet.
- Owner: Tomas Garro

## The short answer

Citizens do not pay. Organisations pay, when they need to hear from people who
are verified, who answer once, and whose names the organisation must not hold.

Three kinds of organisation have that need today. Grants pay for the work
until one of them does.

## What is sold

| Product | What the buyer gets | What the buyer never gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A verified consultation | One answer per verified adult, a public count, and a result anyone can check on-chain | A list of who answered, or how any person answered |
| A sourced brief | What is asked and what was argued, each sentence tied to the official record, reviewed by a named person | Advice on how to answer |
| Evidence | A record that the rules were published before the consultation and were not changed | Personal data to store, secure or disclose |

The third column is the offer. A buyer that holds no personal data has nothing
to leak and less to answer for under data protection law.

## The routes

| Route | Who pays | What they pay for | Price anchor in the market | Time to a first sale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Media | Newsrooms | A brief and a verified reader pulse for each vote date | Unknown. Newsrooms buy opt-in polls today | Weeks |
| Campaigns | Initiative committees, NGOs | A verified signal of support. Later, electronic signatures | A free platform exists (WeCollect) and struggles to fund itself | Weeks to months |
| Organisations | Associations, parties, unions | Private consultations of verified members | ElectionBuddy: USD 29 to 299 per election. POLYAS: from EUR 50 | Days to weeks |
| Government | Cantons, communes, chancelleries | Consultation and e-collecting with data minimisation | Go Vocal: GBP 12,500 to 36,500 a year per licence | 6 to 18 months |
| Grants | Foundations, ecosystem funds | Public-interest infrastructure | Prototype Fund: up to CHF 50,000. Buildathon: 4,000 and 5,000 USDT | A bridge, not a business |
| Citizens | Nobody, now | | | Not a Wave 2 bet |

### What each anchor tells us

| Anchor | Reading |
| --- | --- |
| ElectionBuddy and POLYAS sell a member vote for tens to hundreds | Organisations buy fast and pay little. Volume, not margin |
| Go Vocal sells a municipality a yearly licence in five figures | Government pays well and buys slowly |
| WeCollect is free to committees and asked the public for CHF 250,000 to keep running | Campaign tooling is valued and hard to fund. Charging committees directly is unproven |
| St. Gallen runs the first e-collecting pilot, built by a vendor for the canton | The state buys this from vendors. Identity comes from the state's own login |

## Why Switzerland first

| Fact | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Four federal vote dates a year | A recurring reason to return, and a calendar to sell against |
| Parliamentary debates and votes are open data | The brief can cite the record |
| The first e-collecting pilot started in St. Gallen on 1 September 2026 | Verified, private participation is on the public agenda now |
| The federal e-ID is due on 1 December 2026 | A second credential source, issued by the state |

The product itself is not Swiss. A passport chip works in any country that
issues one. A new country needs a body of evidence for the brief and, later,
its own identity scheme.

## What Wave 2 will test

| Test | Passes if |
| --- | --- |
| A pilot with real people | 20 to 50 verified adults seal an answer, and at least half return to count it |
| Ten conversations opened | Five take place |
| One buyer says yes in writing | One letter of interest, from any route |

## What we do not know

| Unknown | Why it matters | How we find out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The fee for a transaction on mainnet | It is the unit cost of an answer. On Preview it costs nothing | Measure at mainnet launch |
| The cost of one reviewed brief | It is the unit cost of a consultation | Record model spend and review time for the four objects of 29 November |
| Whether a newsroom pays for a verified pulse | The media route rests on it | Ask three newsrooms |
| Whether a canton may buy from a project at this stage | The government route rests on it | Ask one chancellery |
| Whether people return to count their answer | An answer that is not counted is not in the result | The pilot |
