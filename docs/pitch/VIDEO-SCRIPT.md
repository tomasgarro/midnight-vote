# Three-minute video: script and shot list

- Status: draft, 2 October 2026. Not recorded.
- Rule: every shot of the app is real footage of the Preview run on a phone.
  Nothing is shown working that has not worked. A simulated screen carries its
  "Demo" label.
- Format: 16:9 master for the submission, 9:16 cut for social. Narration in a
  natural voice, no stitched pauses. No small grey captions.
- Length: 2:50 to 3:00.

## What must be true before recording

| Shot needs | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| A passport chip read on a phone | The Preview run with a real passport |
| A pass issued | The credential service's receipt, and the registry's count going up |
| An answer sealed from the phone | The consultation's count of sealed answers going up |
| The answer counted by its owner | The tally on the contract after the close |

If a row is not true on the day, cut the shot and say so in the narration.

## Lines that must not be said

- "Your data never leaves your phone." The proof and the sealed answer do
  leave it. Say what is not sent: the name and the passport number.
- "Nobody can ever link it to you." The relayer sees a network address, and
  timing is public. Say what the record does not contain.
- "Anonymous tally." The answer becomes public when it is counted. Who gave it
  does not.
- "Residents" or "citizens of a place". A passport shows nationality.
- Anything about an official vote, a legal signature or an audit.
